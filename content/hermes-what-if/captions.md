## LinkedIn

What if Hermès asked us to build a wellness room in India?

Nobody has. This is a thought experiment, and it's how we approach every brief.

We wouldn't start with a logo, a pop-up or a product wall. We'd start with the house's codes: the horse, the hand, and time. A house that began making harnesses in 1837, and where one artisan still makes a bag from start to finish, can't have a fast room.

So we'd build the evening on the saddle stitch: two needles, one thread, pulled from both sides. In and out. Breath, then heat.

→ The Salt Room. Kutch salt underfoot, raked between sessions into the pattern of a printed scarf. Breathwork, lying down.
→ The Manège. A round cedar heat room shaped like a riding arena, with vetiver steam poured every eight minutes.
→ The Unhurried Hour. Silk, tulsi, kahwa, and nothing to buy.

The product is in every room and on sale in none of them.

What the house would keep: a named format it can run again (a monsoon edition in Mumbai, a winter one in Delhi), a guest list it wrote, and film made for its own channels.

This one is imagined. Yours doesn't have to be.
