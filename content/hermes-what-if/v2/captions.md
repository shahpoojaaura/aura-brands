## LinkedIn

What if Hermès called us to build a wellness room in India?

Nobody has. It's a thought experiment, and it's how we'd approach the brief.

We wouldn't start with a pop-up or a logo on a wall. We'd start with the house's codes: the horse, the hand, and time. So the evening is built on the saddle stitch: two needles, one thread, pulled from both sides. In and out. Breath, then heat.

→ Room one: salt. Breathwork on Kutch salt, with the silk folded under each guest's head.
→ Room two: a round cedar heat room, shaped like a manège, with vetiver steam.
→ Room three: rest. Tulsi, kahwa, a sitar, and nothing to buy.

The product is in every room and on sale in none of them.

What the house keeps: a ritual with a name, a guest list it wrote, and a film of the evening.
